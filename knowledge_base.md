# Ridgeline Plumbing & Drain: Office Knowledge Base

Ridgeline is a fictional company. Everything here is made up for a demo.

## About us
- Local plumbing company serving the Dallas-Fort Worth area.
- Residential and light commercial work (restaurants, offices, small retail).
- Licensed by the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners and fully insured. License number available on request.
- Office phone: (214) 555-0142
- 24/7 emergency line: (214) 555-0199

## Service area
Dallas, Fort Worth, Arlington, Plano, Irving, Garland, Grand Prairie, Mesquite, Carrollton, Richardson, Frisco, McKinney, Allen, and Lewisville.
Outside these cities, the office has to check before we can say yes.

## What we do
- Leaks, burst pipes, and slab leak detection
- Drain cleaning and sewer line clogs, including camera inspections
- Water heaters (tank and tankless): repair and replacement
- Toilets, faucets, sinks, garbage disposals, and shower valves
- Gas line repair and gas leak response
- Repiping and water line replacement
- Water pressure problems and pressure-reducing valves
- Commercial: grease traps (clogs and line jetting, not pumping), restroom fixtures, and backflow testing

## What we don't do
- Septic tank pumping
- Lawn sprinkler and irrigation systems (that needs a licensed irrigator). A sprinkler problem is never a plumbing emergency for us, even if a zone won't shut off: tell the customer to turn off the irrigation shutoff valve (usually near the backflow device) or the main water valve, and to call a licensed irrigator. No fees apply, and no follow-up from our office is needed.
- Pool and spa plumbing
- Well pumps

## Hours
- Office and regular appointments: Monday to Friday, 7 AM to 5 PM.
- Closed Saturday and Sunday, except for emergencies.

## Fees
- Diagnostic visit: $89. A plumber comes out, finds the problem, and gives an upfront price before any work starts. If you go ahead with the repair, the $89 is credited toward it.
- After-hours emergency fee: $150, for emergency calls outside Monday-Friday 7 AM-5 PM. It replaces the diagnostic fee and is not credited toward the repair.
- We never quote a price for a job by email or phone. Every job is priced in person after the plumber sees it.
- Payment: card, check, or cash, due when the work is done. Commercial customers can apply for net-30 billing.

## Scheduling
- We book in two windows: morning (8 AM-12 PM) or afternoon (12-5 PM).
- The office confirms every appointment by phone or text. Nothing is booked until the office confirms.
- The plumber texts about 30 minutes before arriving.
- To book, we need: name, address, best phone number, a short description of the problem, and a preferred window.
- Someone 18 or older needs to be home for the visit.
- Cancelling or rescheduling: please give at least 2 hours' notice. No fee.
- Commercial customers can ask for early-morning or after-close appointments. The office arranges these case by case.

## Warranty
- Labor: guaranteed for 2 years from the date of the work.
- Parts and equipment: covered by the manufacturer's warranty (for example, water heater tanks usually have 6-12 years from the manufacturer).
- Drain clogs caused by what goes down the drain later (grease, wipes, roots) are not covered by the labor warranty.
- To use the warranty, the office pulls up the original job and schedules a return visit. There's no charge for warranty visits.

## Emergencies
An emergency is: water actively leaking or flooding, a burst pipe, no water to the whole house, sewage backing up, or a gas smell.

Safety steps to give right away:
- Water leak or burst pipe: shut off the main water valve. It's usually where the water line enters the house, in the garage, or in a box near the street. Turn off the water heater if the leak is near it.
- Gas smell: leave the building now. Don't flip switches or use flames. From outside, call the gas company or 911. Call us after you're safe.
- Sewage backup: stop using water (no flushing, showers, or laundry) until we arrive.

Then call the emergency line: (214) 555-0199.
